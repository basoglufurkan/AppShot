# Astrology & Tarot

## The searcher

- Curious about love right now: a new crush, a breakup, "are we compatible?" — the single strongest trigger in this niche.
- The daily ritualist who reads their horoscope with morning coffee and wants it to feel personal, not generic.
- The self-explorer who just learned about the "big three" and wants their full birth chart explained.
- For many, tarot and coffee-cup readings are social entertainment — shared with friends, screenshot-worthy.
- After installing they want to feel: "Seen. This was written about *me*." It's entertainment and reflection — keep it that way.

## What wins the install

1. **Personal, not generic** — "based on your exact birth time and place" kills the "horoscopes are vague" objection.
2. **Love compatibility** — two people, one answer: the highest-intent emotional hook.
3. **Daily ritual** — a fresh reading, a daily card, timely alerts for retrogrades and moon phases.
4. **Ask anything** — AI or human astrologer chat; tarot spreads for a specific question.
5. **Beautiful, shareable visuals** — chart wheels and card art people screenshot and post.
6. **Self-understanding** — strengths, patterns, growth framed positively.

## Story (shot by shot)

1. **Hook** — your chart, decoded — show: birth chart wheel with big three — layout: full-bleed — angle: the user's own chart glowing over a star field.
2. **Today** — your day, by the planets — show: personalised daily reading — layout: hero-device — angle: date, transit headline, 2 lines of copy.
3. **Love** — are you two compatible? — show: compatibility result for two signs/charts — layout: duo-devices — angle: two profiles meeting in the middle; score only as computed by the app.
4. **Tarot** — ask the cards — show: a three-card spread (past / present / future) — layout: trio-fan — angle: cards fanned out of the device.
5. **Ask** — answers to your questions — show: chat with AI or human astrologer — layout: chat-bubbles — angle: a supportive, non-predictive reply.
6. **Alerts** — never be caught off guard — show: notifications (retrograde, full moon, transit) — layout: notification-stack — angle: 3 timely alerts.
7. **Profile** — know your big three — show: sun / moon / rising cards — layout: floating-cards — angle: three sign cards orbiting the device.

## Supporting elements

- **Chart wheel** with houses and planet glyphs, rendered from a sample profile.
- **Big three chips**: "Sun {sun} · Moon {moon} · Rising {rising}".
- **Compatibility meter**: "{score}% match" — only the value the app computes for the sample pair.
- **Three-card spread** labelled Past / Present / Future with card names.
- **Notifications**: "Moon enters {sign} tonight", "Mercury retrograde starts {date}".
- **Chat bubbles**: "Should I take the new job?" → a reflective answer that suggests themes, not certainties.
- **Coffee-cup card**: cup photo → reading card.

## Visual direction

- Deep indigo, violet and black with gold line art, star fields and soft aurora gradients; elegant serif display (Cormorant, Playfair) plus a fine sans for UI.
- Two winning styles: mystical-premium (luxe gold, rich gradients) and stark-modern (black-and-white, typographic, Co-Star-like); pick one and keep it across all shots.
- Card art and chart wheels are the hero objects — make them large and crisp.
- Romance angles suit rose/pink gradients.
- Leaders (Co-Star, The Pattern, Nebula, Labyrinthos) make every shot feel personal with a name, date or sign on screen.
- Avoid clip-art zodiac grids; one sign or one chart per shot reads far better.

## Avoid

- Apple 4.3(b) names fortune-telling as a saturated category: reskinned horoscope apps get rejected — screenshots must show what's genuinely different.
- Guaranteed predictions ("Know your future", "100% accurate", "Find out if he'll come back") — misleading under Apple 2.3.1 and Google Play deceptive-behaviour rules.
- Health, pregnancy, legal or financial predictions ("Will I recover?", "When to invest") — potential harm; keep readings to reflection and entertainment.
- Human vs AI ambiguity: label AI readers as AI; never show per-minute prices or coin packs (2.3.7).
- Fabricated testimonials ("It predicted my engagement!"), fake astrologer credentials or user counts.
- Sexual or explicit love content in captions — metadata must suit all audiences (2.3.8).
- Conversion mistakes: a generic 12-sign grid as shot 1 (every competitor has it), tiny glyphs, unreadable script fonts, stock photos of crystal balls.
