# Business & Invoicing

## The searcher

- A freelancer or tradesperson (plumber, photographer, designer, cleaner) who finishes a job and wants to invoice on the spot, from the phone.
- A small business owner chasing late payments and tired of awkward "just following up" messages.
- Someone still making invoices in Word or Excel who wants to look professional instantly.
- After installing they want to feel: professional, organised, and paid on time — with less admin.

## What wins the install

1. **Get paid, not chase** — online payment links and automatic reminders sell the money outcome.
2. **Professional in a minute** — a beautiful branded invoice created on a phone is the proof shot.
3. **Know who owes you** — outstanding/overdue at a glance turns anxiety into a to-do.
4. **Taxes handled** — VAT calculated, compliant formats, e-invoice where supported.
5. **Estimates → invoices** — one tap to convert removes double work.
6. **Tax time ready** — expenses, receipts and reports in one place.

## Supporting elements

- **Invoice card** with a fictional business, clean line items and a "PAID" stamp.
- **Notifications**: "{client} viewed invoice #{number}", "{client} paid {amount}", "Reminder sent for #{number}".
- **KPI tiles**: "{outstanding} outstanding", "{overdueCount} overdue", "{paidThisMonth} paid this month".
- **Template thumbnails**: the same invoice in 3–4 styles.
- **Payment chips**: Card · Bank transfer · Link — only supported methods.
- **Comparison**: "Word template + email" vs "Branded invoice + pay button".
- **Compliance badge** (only if true): "Authorised e-invoice integration via {integrator}".

## Visual direction

- Clean and professional: white/soft-gray backgrounds, one confident brand accent, green reserved for "paid".
- The invoice document is the hero — show it big, crisp and legible; device behind or beside it.
- Modern sans (Inter, Manrope) with tabular figures; avoid accounting-software density.
- Leaders (FreshBooks, Zoho Invoice, Invoice Simple, Wave) show one task per shot and a real-looking small business.
- Tradesperson apps can use on-site photography (van, tools) with the phone; agency/freelancer apps stay minimal.
- Keep currency, date and tax formats local (VAT for UK/EU; sales tax for US).

## Avoid

- Compliance claims you can't back: "fully tax compliant", "HMRC recognised", "MTD ready" — only if officially true.
- Speed-to-payment statistics ("get paid 2× faster") without your own substantiated data (Apple 2.3.1).
- Real client names, tax IDs, addresses or bank details in sample invoices.
- Payment-processor logos (Stripe, PayPal) outside their brand rules; no app pricing or processing fees in screenshots (2.3.7).
- Naming QuickBooks or competitors in captions (2.3.7).
- Conversion mistakes: tiny invoice text, desktop-style tables, accounting jargon on shot 1 ("double-entry ledger"), a settings screen as the hook.
