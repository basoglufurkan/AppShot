# Sports Scores & Live Results

## The searcher

- The fan who can't watch live — at work, in class, travelling — and needs to know the second a goal goes in.
- The stats lover and fantasy player who wants lineups, ratings and xG before anyone else.
- Multi-league followers juggling football, basketball, tennis and F1.
- Diaspora fans following their home team across time zones; in Turkey, Süper Lig and derby-day intensity drive huge spikes.
- After installing they want to feel: never miss a moment, always the first in the group chat to know.

## What wins the install

1. **Fastest, most relevant alerts** — goals, red cards, full time for *my* teams only: the killer feature.
2. **My team front and centre** — personalised home with favourite teams and competitions.
3. **Live match centre** — stats, momentum, play-by-play: watching without a stream.
4. **Lineups and player ratings** — the nerd layer that keeps fans hooked.
5. **Every league in one app** — coverage breadth, named clearly.
6. **Lock Screen and widgets** — live scores without opening anything (Live Activities where supported).

## Story (shot by shot)

1. **Hook** — never miss a goal — show: goal notification + live score on the Lock Screen — layout: notification-stack — angle: stacked goal alert over a Live Activity score.
2. **Your team** — front and centre — show: personalised home with favourite team's next/live match — layout: hero-device — angle: team colours (no crests unless licensed), next fixture card.
3. **Live centre** — every stat that matters — show: live match stats/momentum — layout: zoom-callout — angle: zoom on the momentum graph or xG.
4. **Lineups** — know the XI before kickoff — show: lineup pitch with player ratings — layout: floating-cards — angle: rating badges popping out around the pitch.
5. **Tables & fixtures** — always up to date — show: standings and fixtures — layout: device-top — angle: crop to the top of the table with the user's team highlighted.
6. **All sports** — every league, one app — show: competitions/sports list — layout: screen-grid — angle: sports tiles with league names as text.
7. **News** (optional) — show: transfer news / highlights feed — layout: ui-pieces — angle: one breaking-news card.

## Supporting elements

- **Goal notification**: "GOAL! {teamA} {scoreA}–{scoreB} {teamB} ({minute}')".
- **Live Activity mock** with score, minute and scorer.
- **Momentum chart** or xG bar for a live match.
- **Lineup pitch** with player names (licensed data) and rating badges.
- **Standings snippet**: top 5 with the followed team highlighted.
- **Fixture card**: "{teamA} vs {teamB} · {date} {time}".
- **Breaking news card**: "Transfer confirmed: {player} joins {team}" (only real, licensed news).

## Visual direction

- High energy: dark backgrounds (midnight) with volt or neon accents, or the app's brand colour; condensed bold type (Oswald, Bebas Neue, Barlow Condensed) for scores.
- Big tabular numerals for scores and minutes — they must read in the thumbnail.
- Team identity through colours and plain team names; club crests, league logos and player photos only with licences.
- Leaders (FotMob, SofaScore, OneFootball, theScore, Mackolik in TR) lead with alerts and live match centres, keep tables cropped and readable.
- Blurred stadium photography (licensed) adds atmosphere behind the device.
- Pick a demo match and keep it consistent across shots (same teams, same minute progression).

## Avoid

- Club crests, league logos, competition trophies and player photos without licences — leagues and federations (Premier League, UEFA, TFF) enforce aggressively (Apple 5.2.1, Google Play IP policy).
- "Official app" wording or implied affiliation with a club or league.
- Gambling adjacency: odds, betting tips, "sure wins", bookmaker logos — Apple 5.3 and Google Play's real-money gambling policy; in Turkey illegal betting promotion is a criminal matter (Law 7258).
- "Watch live free" or streaming claims you don't hold rights to — piracy.
- Real, dated results that make the listing look stale; fabricated news or quotes.
- Prices or "free" when alerts are premium (2.3.7).
- Conversion mistakes: a dense standings table as shot 1, 30 league logos, tiny scores, mixing five sports in every shot.
