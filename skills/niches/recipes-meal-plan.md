# Recipes & Meal Planning

## The searcher

- 5 pm, standing at the open fridge: "What's for dinner?" — decision fatigue, not a lack of recipes.
- A busy parent who wants a week of family meals planned and a shopping list done.
- The meal-prepper saving money and time with Sunday batch cooking.
- The home cook collecting recipes from Instagram, TikTok and blogs and wanting them clean and in one place.
- In Turkey: "akşama ne pişirsem?" is a daily search. After installing they want to feel: decided, capable in the kitchen, less food wasted.

## What wins the install

1. **Dinner, decided** — fast, appealing ideas solve the real pain (deciding), not "access to recipes".
2. **Cook with what you have** — ingredient-based suggestions reduce waste and shopping trips.
3. **Plan the week + auto shopping list** — the time- and money-saving loop for families and meal-preppers.
4. **Save from anywhere** — clean recipe cards from any website or social post, without ads and life stories.
5. **Cook mode** — big steps, built-in timers, screen stays on: hands-free confidence.
6. **Fits your diet** — vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, quick, air fryer filters.

## Supporting elements

- **Ingredient chips**: Tomatoes · Eggs · Rice → "{count} recipes you can make".
- **Recipe meta chips**: "{minutes} min · {servings} servings · Easy".
- **Cook-mode timer**: big countdown with step number "Step {n} of {total}".
- **Meal-plan week cards** with small dish photos.
- **Auto list card**: grouped ingredients (Produce, Dairy, Pantry) with check boxes.
- **Diet badges**: Vegan · Gluten-free · High-protein · Air fryer.
- **Before/after**: ad-heavy blog page vs clean recipe card.

## Visual direction

- Appetising, natural-light photography (warm, top-down or 45°, real textures) on cream/editorial backgrounds; ember or sunset accents for warmth, forest for healthy/plant-based.
- Food-magazine typography: a characterful serif for headlines, a clean sans for UI and quantities.
- Full-bleed hero dishes; device frames smaller and overlapping the photo.
- Leaders: Mob and Kitchen Stories (editorial, bold photography), Tasty (bright, video-led), Paprika (utility), Nefis Yemek Tarifleri (TR community).
- Localise dishes per storefront (mercimek çorbası and karnıyarık for TR, tacos and lasagna for US).
- Keep step text huge in cook-mode shots — readability is the selling point.

## Avoid

- Health and weight-loss claims ("lose 5 kg with this plan", "anti-inflammatory", "cures bloating") — Apple 1.4.1 and Google Play health-claim rules; diet labels only when computed and accurate.
- "Allergy-safe" guarantees — allergen info is guidance, never a promise.
- Copyrighted photos or recipes from blogs, cookbooks or influencers; other platforms' branding visible in imported recipes.
- AI-generated food images presented as real results.
- Grocery prices or supermarket logos (2.3.7, 5.2.1).
- Conversion mistakes: an ingredient list as shot 1, dark or cluttered food photos, tiny step text, ten recipe cards per shot.
