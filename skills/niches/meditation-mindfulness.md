# Meditation & Mindfulness

## The searcher

- Someone stressed and overwhelmed right now, often searching in the moment of need ("calm down", "stress relief", "breathing").
- Someone who can't switch off at night and wants guided sleep content.
- A curious beginner who believes "I can't meditate, my mind is too busy" and wants short, guided sessions.
- A returning meditator looking for a timer, courses or a fresh voice.
- What they want to feel: "Calmer within minutes, and like I can actually do this."

## What wins the install

1. **Immediate relief.** 1–5 minute sessions answer "I don't have time / I can't sit still", the core objection.
2. **Beginner-friendly guidance.** A "start here" course signals no experience needed.
3. **Breadth for life's moments.** Stress, sleep, focus, gratitude. People install for one need and stay for the library.
4. **Calming aesthetics.** The screenshots themselves should lower the viewer's heart rate, and that feeling is the proof.
5. **Voices and languages.** Native-language narration is a big differentiator in non-English markets.
6. **A gentle habit.** Mindful minutes and soft streaks, without guilt.

## Story (shot by shot)

1. **Hook** — instant calm — show: serene nature image with one large calm caption and a subtle play control — layout: text-hero — angle: feel calmer in {minutes} minutes.
2. **Breathe** — the simplest tool — show: breathing animation circle with inhale/hold/exhale — layout: hero-device — angle: breathe in, let go.
3. **Library** — breadth — show: categories (Stress, Sleep, Focus, Gratitude, Anxious moments) as tiles — layout: screen-grid — angle: calm for every moment.
4. **Beginner course** — confidence — show: "Day 1 of 7: Learn to breathe" course cards — layout: floating-cards — angle: new to meditation? Start here.
5. **Sleep** — second audience — show: full-bleed night scene with a sleep story player — layout: full-bleed — angle: fall asleep to soothing stories.
6. **Habit** — close — show: mindful minutes, gentle reminders, offline downloads, narrators — layout: feature-list — angle: make calm a daily habit.

## Supporting elements

- Breathing circle: "Inhale 4 · Hold 4 · Exhale 4", with a soft expanding ring.
- Session card: "{minutes} min · Stress · {narrator}", with a cover illustration.
- Mood check-in chips: Stressed, Tired, Restless, Calm, plus a recommended session.
- Mindful minutes stat: "{minutes} mindful minutes this week".
- Gentle reminder: "Take a 3-minute pause".
- Soft streak dots (not flames or trophies).
- Sleep story cover tiles with narrator name and length.

## Visual direction

- Calm, aurora, sunset and pastel palettes: soft gradients, mist, water, sky, illustrated landscapes. Lots of empty space.
- Typography: light serif or soft rounded sans, sentence case, small airy captions. One idea per shot, and fewer words than any other niche.
- Keep the device small and centered, or hidden in favor of mood. The breathing animation works as a standalone UI piece.
- Leaders use illustrated scenery, big calm color fields and very few words. Busy, high-contrast screenshots contradict the promise.

## Avoid

- Clinical claims: "treats anxiety", "cures depression", "clinically proven" without published evidence. Apple 1.4.1 and Google Play's health misinformation policy apply. Wellbeing framing only ("feel calmer", "unwind").
- Therapy language ("your therapist", "therapy sessions") for self-guided content.
- Religious or spiritual symbols used decoratively, which can alienate parts of the market. Keep spiritual content opt-in and clearly labeled.
- Celebrity narrators' names or photos unless licensed (Apple 2.3.9).
- Prices, trials, "free" or fabricated "{users} meditators" figures.
- Conversion killers: cluttered layouts, more than one line of text per shot, and aggressive gamification visuals (trophies, flames) that clash with calm.
