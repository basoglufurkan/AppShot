# Podcasts & Audio Stories

## The searcher

- Two app types share this niche — identify which one this is:
- **Podcast players**: commuters, gym-goers and dish-washers who follow many shows and want a better player than the default — smart speed, silence skipping, chapters, transcripts.
- **Serialised audio fiction** (romance, thriller, fantasy series): binge listeners hooked on a cliffhanger, wanting the next episode *now*.
- Bedtime listeners using stories and calm shows to fall asleep.
- After installing they want to feel: never bored, never lost in their queue, pulled into the next episode.

## What wins the install

1. **The story hook** (audio fiction) — a dramatic cover and one-line logline sell harder than any feature.
2. **Never miss an episode** (players) — follow, auto-download, notifications: the core loop.
3. **Player superpowers** — speed, skip silence, chapters, transcripts make listening better than elsewhere.
4. **Discovery by interest** — genres, charts, picks that match taste.
5. **Offline and bedtime** — downloads for flights and commutes; sleep timer for nights.
6. **Pick up anywhere** — synced progress across phone, car and speakers.

## Supporting elements

- **Cover art** (original or licensed) with a one-line logline card for fiction series.
- **Speed chip**: "1.5x"; **Skip-silence badge**: "Saved {minutes} min" from real listening stats.
- **Transcript highlight** on the sentence being spoken.
- **Notifications**: "New episode of {show}", "Episode {n} is ready — the twist continues".
- **Episode card**: "Ep {n} · {minutes} min" with progress bar.
- **Chapter list** with timestamps.
- **Sleep timer chip**: "End of episode".

## Visual direction

- Audio fiction: cinematic and emotional — midnight, rose or sunset palettes, dramatic character covers (all-ages appropriate), bold serif or condensed titles.
- Podcast players: editorial and clean — Overcast/Pocket Casts/Castro style, artwork-driven colour, generous whitespace.
- Covers are the hero; let them fill the frame or fan out (trio-fan).
- Waveforms and transcript highlights communicate "audio" without microphone clichés.
- Show one series or one show per shot; consistent cover style across the set.
- Readability first: dark themes need bright, high-contrast captions.

## Avoid

- Other shows' artwork and names as hero content without permission — trademark and copyright (Apple 5.2.1); use your own or licensed series.
- Mature or sexual themes in covers and captions — metadata must suit all audiences even when content is rated higher (Apple 2.3.8).
- Coin packs, per-episode unlock prices or "free" when episodes are gated (Apple 2.3.1, 2.3.7, 3.1.1).
- Fabricated listener counts, chart positions or awards.
- Podcast players: naming Apple Podcasts or Spotify in captions (2.3.7).
- Conversion mistakes: a list of episode titles as the hook, generic microphone photos, tiny covers, mixing ten genres in one shot.
