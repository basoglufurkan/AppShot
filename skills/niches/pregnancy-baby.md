# Pregnancy & Baby Tracker

## The searcher

- Newly pregnant, excited and a bit anxious, wanting to know "what's happening this week". They search "pregnancy tracker" or "pregnancy week by week".
- A partner who wants to follow along and feel involved.
- A new parent at 3 a.m. who needs to log feeds, sleep and diapers one-handed and remember which side was last.
- What they want to feel: "Reassured, connected to my baby, and carrying less mental load."

This niche spans pregnancy and newborn care. Lead with the app's primary phase. The story below is pregnancy-first. For baby trackers, open with shot 5.

## What wins the install

1. **Emotional connection.** "Your baby is the size of a {fruit}" or a 3D baby visual is the iconic hook of the category, and it's deeply personal.
2. **Trustworthy content.** Week-by-week guidance reviewed by qualified professionals (only if true) answers "can I trust this?"
3. **Practical tools.** Kick counter, contraction timer, hospital bag checklist. Concrete help at key moments.
4. **One-handed newborn logging.** Big buttons and timers for feeds, sleep and diapers, built for exhausted parents.
5. **Partner and family sharing.** Synced logs and shared updates turn a solo app into a family one.
6. **Memories.** Bump photos and "firsts" make it a keepsake, not just a utility.

## Supporting elements

- Size card: fruit or 3D illustration with "{lengthCm} cm · {weightG} g", using averages from a cited source.
- Countdown chip: "{daysLeft} days to go" and a trimester progress bar.
- Kick counter button with "{kicks} kicks · {minutes} min".
- Contraction timer table: duration and interval rows.
- Feed log row: "Left side · {minutes} min · {time}" with a "last side" indicator.
- Partner sync chip: two avatars with "Synced with {partnerName}".
- Hospital bag checklist card and milestone stickers ("First smile").

## Visual direction

- Pastel, cream, sky and rose palettes: soft, warm and reassuring, with gentle illustrations and real, warm photos of parents (licensed models).
- Typography: rounded friendly sans, generous spacing, short captions. Tired parents skim.
- Baby imagery: licensed model photos or illustrations only. Avoid anything that looks like a real ultrasound scan.
- Leaders use 3D baby renders or fruit illustrations, soft gradients, week counters and partner sharing. Clinical charts belong deep in the set, if anywhere.

## Avoid

- Medical advice or diagnosis ("know if something is wrong", "check your baby's health"). Under Apple 1.4.1, health content needs credible sources and should remind users to consult their doctor or midwife.
- Due-date or development certainty. Use "estimated" and "on average".
- Fear-based copy (miscarriage risk, SIDS scares) in marketing.
- Real babies' or children's photos, names or birth details without explicit consent. Children's data and privacy (COPPA, GDPR) apply even when the user is the parent.
- Images that look like real ultrasound scans, implying imaging or diagnostic capability.
- Fabricated "reviewed by OB-GYN" badges, invented ratings or "{users} moms" counts.
- Prices, trials or affiliate product deals in screenshots.
- Conversion killers: clinical charts in shot 1, ignoring partners, and generic stock photos that feel cold.
