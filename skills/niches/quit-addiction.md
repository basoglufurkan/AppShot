# Quit & Recovery

## The searcher

- Someone who decided today (or again) to quit smoking, vaping or drinking and wants a counter and support from day one. They search "quit smoking app" or "sober counter".
- Someone already a few days or weeks in, fighting cravings, who needs help in the hard moments.
- Someone who slipped and is restarting, and is sensitive to shame.
- People quitting other habits (gambling, sugar) who look for the same counter-plus-support mechanics.
- What they want to feel: "Proof I'm winning, help when it's hard, and no judgment."

## What wins the install

1. **A visible victory counter.** Days, hours and minutes since quitting is concrete, motivating and the iconic image of the niche.
2. **Money and time saved.** A tangible reward people can picture (a trip, a gift) from the money they're no longer spending.
3. **Help with cravings in the moment.** An SOS button with breathing or distraction tools is the feature that keeps people from relapsing, and the one they install for.
4. **A health timeline.** What improves over time, shown as sourced, educational milestones. Hopeful and motivating.
5. **Non-judgmental restarts.** "A slip doesn't erase your progress" kills fear of failure, a major barrier.
6. **Community and accountability.** Not doing it alone, through a partner, group or shared milestones.

## Supporting elements

- Big live counter: "{days}d {hours}h {minutes}m" with "Quit on {quitDate}".
- Money saved card: "{currency}{saved} saved". This is computed from the user's own inputs, not app pricing.
- Avoided count: "{units} cigarettes not smoked" or "{units} drinks skipped".
- Health timeline rows with approximate timings and an in-app source citation (for example a national health service).
- Craving SOS button that opens a breathing circle with "This craving will pass. Breathe with me".
- Notification: "{days} days! You unlocked a new milestone".
- Reasons card: "My reasons: {reason}", with an optional photo of what matters (licensed or illustrated).

## Visual direction

- Forest, sky, calm and mint palettes: fresh air, open sky, clean water, sunrise. Hopeful, never gloomy.
- Bold rounded numerals for counters, with short warm captions written in an encouraging coach voice.
- Never show cigarettes, vapes or alcohol attractively. If they appear, use a minimal crossed-out icon. No diseased-lung shock imagery.
- Leaders use green gradients, huge day counters, money-saved cards and achievement badges. Add the SOS shot. It's what differentiates a support app from a counter.

## Avoid

- Cure or guarantee claims: "cure your addiction", "quit in 7 days guaranteed", "100% success rate". Addiction is a medical condition, so Apple 1.4.1 and Google Play's health policy apply. Medical claims need evidence and may trigger review.
- Any imagery that glamorizes tobacco, vapes, drugs or alcohol. Apple 1.4.3 prohibits encouraging their consumption, and Google Play restricts it too.
- Promoting nicotine replacement brands, prescription drugs or pharmacological claims.
- Health timeline facts without a credible source, or stated more precisely than the evidence supports.
- Cold-turkey encouragement for heavy alcohol use. Withdrawal can be dangerous, so the app should advise talking to a doctor, and screenshots must never discourage medical help.
- Shame, fear or gross-out imagery, which drives viewers away and draws reviewer attention.
- Fabricated testimonials, sobriety counts or "{users} people quit with us" numbers. App prices or trials in screenshots.
- Conversion killers: gloomy palettes, counters with no support features, and preachy copy.
