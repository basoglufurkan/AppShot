# Shopping & Marketplace

## The searcher

- Two very different users live here — decide which one the app serves first:
- **The shopper**: browsing for inspiration (fashion, home, beauty) or hunting a specific item; wants great finds, fair deals and painless returns.
- **The seller** (C2C marketplaces): decluttering a wardrobe or flat, turning stuff into cash; wants listing to be fast and selling to be safe.
- Both carry the same fear: scams, fakes and lost parcels. Shoppers compare against giant platforms, so trust and niche focus decide.
- After installing they want to feel: "I found something I love" (shopper) or "That sold quickly and safely" (seller).

## What wins the install

1. **Discovery that feels personal** — a curated feed of beautiful products in shot 1 beats category lists.
2. **Trust** — buyer protection, verified sellers, easy returns, secure payments: kills the #1 objection.
3. **Sell in a snap** (C2C) — photo → auto-filled listing in seconds is the seller's magic moment.
4. **Deals without spam** — wishlist and price-drop alerts beat loud discount banners.
5. **Delivery certainty** — tracked shipping, clear status, simple returns.
6. **Human connection** (C2C) — chat and offers make negotiation easy and safe.

## Story (shot by shot)

1. **Hook** — find what you love / sell it in a snap — show: personalised product feed (or listing creation for C2C) — layout: trio-fan — angle: three beautiful product cards fanning out of the device.
2. **Discover** — styles picked for you — show: category/brand/style browsing — layout: screen-grid — angle: product tiles with strong photography, no price emphasis.
3. **List** (C2C) — snap, list, sold — show: photo → AI-filled listing — layout: ui-pieces — angle: photo, auto title, category chips.
4. **Negotiate** — make an offer, safely — show: buyer–seller chat with an offer — layout: chat-bubbles — angle: "Is it still available?" → "Yes! Send an offer".
5. **Alerts** — never miss a drop — show: notifications (price dropped, back in stock, item sold) — layout: notification-stack — angle: three short, specific alerts.
6. **Trust** — shop with protection — show: buyer-protection / returns info — layout: feature-list — angle: protection, verified sellers, returns, secure pay — only what's true.
7. **Track** — every order, every step — show: order tracking — layout: floating-cards — angle: shipped → out for delivery → delivered.

## Supporting elements

- **Product cards** with strong photography, wishlist heart and a "Price dropped" chip (no amounts).
- **Notifications**: "An item on your wishlist is back", "Your {item} sold!", "Your order is out for delivery".
- **Chat bubbles**: buyer question → seller reply → "Offer sent" pill.
- **Listing auto-fill**: photo → title, category, condition chips.
- **Trust badges** (true only): "Buyer protection", "Verified seller", "Secure payment", "Free returns" (only if unconditional).
- **Seller rating**: "★ {sellerRating} · {sales} sales" from real platform data.
- **Tracking steps**: Ordered → Shipped → Delivered.

## Visual direction

- Product photography is the hero: clean backgrounds, consistent lighting, lots of white (clean-light) with the brand colour as accent.
- Fashion and lifestyle stores go editorial (serif headlines, rose/cream palettes, model-free flat lays or licensed model shots).
- C2C marketplaces go friendly and approachable (teal, green, volt accents), with real-looking user photos taken by the team.
- Leaders: Vinted and Depop (community, C2C), Etsy (handmade, warm), Zara/H&M apps (editorial).
- Avoid the discount-app look (red/yellow explosions, countdowns) unless that's genuinely the brand — it reads spammy.
- Keep product grids to 4–6 large tiles; crop with device-top when needed.

## Avoid

- Brand logos and designer products you're not authorised to sell; anything that could look counterfeit (Apple 5.2.1/5.2.2, Google Play IP policy).
- Strike-through "was/now" prices, "90% off", fake countdown timers — EU Omnibus rules require the prior lowest price; misleading under Apple 2.3.1. No prices in captions (2.3.7).
- "Free shipping" or "free returns" when conditional.
- Prohibited or restricted goods in demo listings (weapons, alcohol, tobacco, medicines, adult items) — screenshots must suit all audiences (Apple 2.3.8).
- Models or user photos without releases; real users' names and ratings presented as testimonials.
- Conversion mistakes: tiny grids of 20 products, a promo-banner carousel as shot 1, inconsistent photography, no trust story for C2C.
