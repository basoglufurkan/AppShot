# Food Delivery

## The searcher

- Hungry *now* — decides in seconds, driven by appetite and convenience, not features.
- The office worker ordering lunch at the desk; the family solving tonight's dinner; the late-night craver.
- A loyal customer of a specific restaurant or chain installing its own ordering app (single-brand apps live here too).
- In Turkey: a market dominated by big platforms — challengers win on speed, local favourites or a specific cuisine.
- After installing they want to feel: hot food is coming, they know exactly when, and it was effortless.

## What wins the install

1. **Appetite** — mouth-watering food photography is the first and strongest conversion lever.
2. **Speed and certainty** — live courier tracking and an honest ETA remove the "where is my food?" anxiety.
3. **Choice nearby** — favourite cuisines and local spots, all in one place.
4. **Effortless reorder** — one tap to repeat your usual.
5. **Make it yours** — customise every item (extra cheese, no onions) without phoning.
6. **Rewards** — loyalty and offers as a concept, never with numbers in screenshots.

## Story (shot by shot)

1. **Hook** — your cravings, delivered — show: hero dish photo with the app's restaurant/menu card — layout: full-bleed — angle: top-down steaming dish filling the frame, UI card overlapping.
2. **Choice** — every cuisine nearby — show: cuisine/restaurant discovery — layout: screen-grid — angle: appetising tiles (pizza, burger, sushi, kebab).
3. **Customise** — exactly how you like it — show: item customisation sheet — layout: zoom-callout — angle: zoom on add-ons and the "Add to basket" button (no price emphasis).
4. **Track** — know exactly when it arrives — show: live map with courier and ETA — layout: hero-device — angle: courier icon on the route, big ETA chip.
5. **Updates** — every step, in real time — show: order-status notifications — layout: notification-stack — angle: confirmed → preparing → on the way.
6. **Reorder** — your usual in one tap — show: past orders/favourites — layout: ui-pieces — angle: reorder button lifted out.
7. **Rewards** (optional) — show: loyalty/stamps screen — layout: floating-cards — angle: stamp card progress, no amounts.

## Supporting elements

- **Courier on map** with an ETA chip "{eta} min" and the courier's first name.
- **Status notifications**: "Order confirmed", "Your food is being prepared", "Courier is {distance} away".
- **Cuisine chips**: Pizza · Burger · Sushi · Kebab · Vegan · Dessert.
- **Restaurant card** with in-app rating "★ {rating}" — only real ratings from the platform's data.
- **Reorder pill**: "Order your usual again".
- **Customisation chips**: Extra cheese · No onions · Spicy.
- **Loyalty stamp card**: "{stamps}/10" without monetary values.

## Visual direction

- Appetite colours: reds, oranges and warm yellows (ember, sunset) or the brand's own colour; high-saturation food photography, top-down or 45° with visible steam and texture.
- Full-bleed food behind or around the device; the UI should look clean and fast against rich photography.
- Bold, rounded or condensed type; short captions that sound like cravings.
- Leaders (DoorDash, Uber Eats, Deliveroo, Getir, Yemeksepeti) make the food the hero in shot 1 and the tracking map the hero in shot 3–4.
- Photograph dishes that partner restaurants actually serve; consistent lighting across all shots.
- Avoid dark, muddy food shots and busy tablecloth backgrounds.

## Avoid

- Prices, discount percentages, "free delivery" or minimum-order details in screenshots — they change and can mislead (Apple 2.3.1, 2.3.7).
- Delivery-time guarantees ("in 10 minutes") unless always true in the shown market.
- Chain logos and branded packaging (McDonald's, Burger King, Starbucks) without a partnership (Apple 5.2.1).
- Stock or AI-generated food that doesn't match what partners actually serve.
- Alcohol: online sale of alcoholic drinks is banned in Turkey (Law 4250 as amended by Law 6487), and elsewhere it requires age gating and the right age rating — keep it out of TR screenshots.
- Fake ratings or "#1 delivery app".
- Conversion mistakes: an address/login screen as shot 1, grids of tiny restaurant logos, captions longer than the craving lasts.
