# Bible & Christian Prayer

## The searcher

- A believer who wants a daily rhythm — a verse with morning coffee, a prayer before sleep — and keeps falling out of the habit.
- Someone in a hard season (anxiety, grief, illness, a broken relationship) looking for comfort and words to pray.
- A new or returning believer who wants to understand what they read, with plans and explanations.
- Seasonal surges: Lent, Holy Week and Easter; Advent and Christmas; the new year, when Bible-in-a-year plans start.
- After installing they want to feel: "Closer, calmer, consistent." Match the app's own tradition and vocabulary (Bible/verse/devotional for Protestant apps, rosary/novena/saints for Catholic apps, prayer rule/akathist for Orthodox apps) — never blend them unless the app is ecumenical. Islamic prayer-time, qibla and Quran apps use `islamic-prayer.md`.

## What wins the install

1. **A daily rhythm that's easy to keep** — verse of the day, reading plans, gentle reminders: the core job is consistency.
2. **Comfort in the moment** — prayers and passages for anxiety, sleep, gratitude, loss.
3. **Reverent, beautiful reading** — clean typography and calm design signal respect and trustworthiness.
4. **Authenticity and accuracy** — trusted translations (KJV, NIV, ESV, NABRE, Douay-Rheims…) and correct references.
5. **Audio** — listen to Scripture or recitation on the commute or before sleep.
6. **Community and family** — shared plans, prayer requests, praying for each other.

## Supporting elements

- **Verse card**: verse text + exact reference (e.g. "Psalm 23:1") in the translation the app actually licenses.
- **Plan progress**: "Day {day} of {total} · {planName}".
- **Notifications**: "Verse of the day is ready", "Time for evening prayer", "{name} prayed for your request".
- **Streak**: "{days} days in the Word".
- **Community bubble**: a short, respectful prayer request with "Praying" count {count}.

## Visual direction

- Serene and reverent: cream/parchment or deep midnight, gold or warm accents (luxe), plenty of space; serif text faces (Cormorant, EB Garamond, Lora).
- Natural light photography — sunrise, mountains, still water — rather than kitschy illustrations; keep imagery humble.
- Catholic/Orthodox apps can use sacred art only with rights; Hallow-style apps go dark and premium.
- Verse text must be large enough to read in search results — one verse per shot, never a wall of chapters.
- Calm motion cues (light rays, soft gradients) beat badges, stickers and loud colours.

## Avoid

- Disrespect of any kind: humour, clickbait, sacred text over inappropriate imagery, cropped or broken verses — Apple 1.1.1 explicitly covers mean-spirited religious commentary; reviews in this niche punish carelessness hard.
- Wrong references or a verse that doesn't match the stated translation; copyrighted translations (NIV, ESV, NKJV, NABRE and many modern versions) need a licence even in screenshots.
- Promises of miracles, healing, wealth or answered prayers ("This prayer heals", "Attract abundance") — misleading and harmful under Apple 1.4.1/2.3.1 and Google Play's deceptive-behaviour policy.
- Church or denomination endorsement you don't have ("Official app of…", a diocese crest, a church logo) — impersonation under Apple 5.2.1 and Play's impersonation policy.
- Donation or tithe screens — Apple 3.2.1(vi) restricts in-app donations to approved nonprofits; keep payments out of screenshots.
- Fabricated testimonies ("This app saved my marriage") or invented community counts.
- Conversion mistakes: a stock "praying hands" photo with no UI, tiny verse text, mixing traditions, dark low-contrast night themes on every shot.
