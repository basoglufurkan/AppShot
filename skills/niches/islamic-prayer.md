# Islamic Prayer & Quran

## The searcher

- A practising Muslim who wants **correct times for where they are** and an ezan alert they can trust. They search at
  the moment of need ("namaz vakti", "ezan vakti", "prayer times") and often type without Turkish letters ("kible",
  "kuran", "imsakiye").
- A traveller, student or expat in an unfamiliar city who needs local times and the qibla fast, often offline.
  Turkey is the key market, and the Turkish listing also reaches the large diaspora in Germany, the Netherlands,
  Austria and France, because the App Store shows the device's language.
- Someone building a Quran habit: reading daily, finishing a hatim in Ramazan, listening on the commute, and
  understanding through a trusted Turkish meal.
- A seasonal wave around Ramazan (imsakiye, iftar and sahur countdowns, teravih), the kandil nights, Cuma and the
  bayram days. Search interest climbs in the weeks before Ramazan, so seasonal pages need lead time.
- A wide age range. Older users want large, simple screens. Younger users want widgets, the lock screen and Apple
  Watch. Both want calm: an ad popping up during worship is a frequent complaint in this niche's reviews.
- After installing they want to feel "Vaktim elimde, içim huzurlu": sure of the time, calm, and not distracted.
  Scope: prayer times (namaz vakitleri, ezan), qibla, Quran (meal, tefsir, hatim), dua, dhikr (zikir/tesbih) and
  Ramazan (imsakiye). Christian apps use `bible-prayer.md`.

## What wins the install

1. **Accuracy you can trust.** Times for my location using the method I follow (Diyanet's published times in Turkey,
   or MWL, ISNA, Umm al-Qura and others abroad) and an ezan that arrives on time. For most users this is the whole
   product.
2. **A qibla that works.** A clear compass with a calibration hint, plus a map or AR view if the app has one.
3. **A reverent Quran reader.** Clean Arabic script, a meal alongside, okunuş (transliteration), recitation audio, and
   cüz/sure navigation that resumes where you left off.
4. **Ramazan tools.** Imsakiye, iftar countdown, a sahur alarm, and hatim tracking for the month.
5. **Calm and respect.** Ad-light or ad-free (only if true), sober design, nothing gamified about worship.
6. **Glanceability.** Next-prayer countdown in widgets, on the lock screen and on the Watch.
7. **Privacy.** Location used on the device, no account needed (only if true). Religious data is sensitive, and users
   notice.

1. **Hook** — correct times for your city, next vakit highlighted — show: today's vakitler with the countdown to the next one — layout: hero-device — angle: Şehrine göre doğru namaz vakitleri, no account needed.
2. **Ezan** — never miss a vakit — show: today screen with two ezan notifications on top — layout: notification-stack — angle: pre-alerts and the ezan sound you choose.

5. **Hatim** — keep a steady rhythm — show: hatim or reading-plan progress with 2 cards ({juz}. cüz, last ayah) — layout: floating-cards — angle: kaldığın yerden devam.
6. **Zikir and dua** (evergreen) or **Ramazan** (seasonal page) — show: zikirmatik and tesbihat crop, or the iftar countdown and imsakiye row — layout: ui-pieces — angle: tesbihat adım adım / iftara kaç dakika kaldı.

## Supporting elements

- **Vakit strip** (in the captured UI): İmsak · Güneş · Öğle · İkindi · Akşam · Yatsı with {times} computed **by the
  app** for the stated sample city and date. Record the city, date and method in `brief.md`.
- **Countdown card**: "Akşam ezanına {minutes} dk" / "Next: Maghrib in {minutes} min".
- **Notifications**: "Öğle ezanına 15 dakika" · "Akşam ezanı okunuyor · {city}" · "İftara {minutes} dakika kaldı"
  · "Sahur için {time}'de uyandırayım mı?". Keep them to features the app really has, and use no emoji.
- **Qibla card**: "{bearing}° · Kâbe'ye {distance} km" from the app's real calculation.
- **Quran card**: a complete ayah in Arabic with its meal and reference (for example "Bakara, 2:255"), in the meal
  the app is licensed to show.
- **Hatim progress**: "{juz}. cüz · %{percent}" · "Son okunan: {sure}, {ayah}".
- **Zikirmatik**: "{count}/33 · Sübhânallah", plus the tesbihat sequence.
- **Imsakiye row**: "Ramazan {day}. gün · İmsak {time} · İftar {time}" (seasonal page only).
- **Method chips**: "Diyanet vakitleri", "Hanefi ikindi", "MWL" (only the methods the app supports).

## Visual direction

- **Themes:** `forest` (deep green and cream serif, the classic look), `luxe` (black and gold, ideal for Ramazan and
  Quran sets), `calm` (night indigo with stars for imsak, teravih and Kadir Gecesi), `cream` (parchment for Quran
  reading), `midnight` (modern premium), `soft-gray` (utility prayer-time apps that should feel native). Use one
  accent, gold or green.
- **Typography:** a serif display face (Fraunces, Playfair, DM Serif) for Quran and Ramazan, a calm grotesk for
  prayer-time utilities. All theme fonts cover Turkish. Keep Arabic **inside the captured UI**, where the app renders
  it correctly. Don't type Arabic into Turkish or English captions: it falls back to a system face at tight
  leading and can clip harakat.
- **Decor:** restrained. `glow` behind the device, `rings` behind the qibla compass, the faint `grid` or `dots`
  for a geometric hint, `sparkles` (as stars) only on `calm`. Never `confetti`, `shapes` or `beams`.
- **Imagery:** no figurative depictions of prophets or companions. No stock photos of people praying. Kaaba and
  mosque photos only with rights. Calm night skies and architecture details beat kitsch.
- **Sacred text:** complete ayahs with correct harakat and reference. Never let a `zoom.rect` or `ui-pieces` crop cut
  through an ayah. Never put `**highlight**` on Allah's names or ayah text: highlight the benefit word
  ("**vakti**", "**kıbleyi**").
- **Device:** upright (`hero-device`, `device-top`). Avoid tilts, because they feel playful here. For Play use
  `storeOverrides.android.theme.device.frame = "flat"`.
- **Light and dark:** many users run the app in dark mode for night prayers. Match the capture to the theme and check
  both modes in `review/search-tr.png`.

## Avoid

- **Religious authority claims:** "Diyanet onaylı", "resmi uygulama", "en doğru vakitler", "sahih", rulings or fatwas in
  captions. Mention Diyanet only as the real data source ("Diyanet vakitleriyle"), only if the app actually uses
  Diyanet's published times, and never so that it implies endorsement (Apple 5.2.1, Play impersonation policy).
- **Spiritual promises:** "kabul olan dua", "sevap kazan", "bu duayı okuyana…", "dileğin gerçekleşir", healing,
  wealth or protection claims. These are misleading under Apple 2.3.1 and 1.4.1 and Play's deceptive-behaviour policy,
  and offensive to many users.
- **Inaccuracy:** times that don't match the app's calculation for the shown city and date, a qibla without a real
  computation, or a method the app doesn't offer. Reviews in this niche punish one wrong vakit hard.
- **Sectarian or regional bias:** mocking or excluding other traditions. Show only the madhab and method options the
  app really supports (Hanefi/Şafii ikindi, Ja'fari). Keep the language inclusive.
- **Gamifying worship:** 🔥 streaks, trophies, "level up your iman", confetti, leaderboards of who prayed most. Use
  quiet progress (checkmarks, "düzenli") instead, and no emoji in captions or notifications.
- **Privacy:** religious belief is special-category data (GDPR Art. 9, KVKK Art. 6 *özel nitelikli kişisel veri*).
  Use fictional demo data only: no real names in dua requests, no real home location, no prayer logs of a real
  person. Claims like "Konumun cihazında kalır" or "Hesap gerekmez" only if true and consistent with the App Privacy
  label and Play Data safety form.
- **Copyright:** many meal translations, recitation recordings and mushaf fonts are licensed. Show only what the app
  has rights to, even in screenshots.
- **Money:** zekât, sadaka or donation screens (Apple 3.2.1(vi) limits in-app donations to approved nonprofits),
  prices, and "ücretsiz" on Play. If ads are removed only in Pro, tag the shot "Pro" (Apple 2.3.2).
- **Seasonal copy in the evergreen set:** Ramazan, kandil or bayram lines belong on a custom product page or a timed
  update (Ramazan 2027 is around early February to early March, so confirm the official calendar). Swap them back
  right after.
- **Captured ads:** never capture an ad banner, interstitial or debug state.
- **Mixing traditions** or Christian vocabulary unless the app is interfaith. Christian apps belong in `bible-prayer.md`.
