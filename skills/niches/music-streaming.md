# Music Streaming & Players

## The searcher

- Several app types share this niche — streaming services, local/offline players, equalizers and boosters, lyrics/karaoke, radio. Match the story to the real one.
- Someone who wants music without interruptions — on a flight, the metro, the gym, with poor signal.
- The audiophile who cares about lossless files, EQ and gapless playback for their own library.
- The discoverer bored of the same playlists, looking for new music that fits their mood.
- After installing they want to feel: their soundtrack, their way — music that fits the moment.

## What wins the install

1. **Mood and identity** — music is emotional; shot 1 must feel like a vibe, not a settings page.
2. **Discovery** — personalised mixes and moods answer "what should I listen to next?"
3. **Uninterrupted listening** — offline playback, own library, no gaps (for players with local files).
4. **Sound you control** — EQ, presets, bass, lossless support.
5. **Sing along** — time-synced lyrics (where licensed) are a strong emotional hook.
6. **Beautiful player everywhere** — Lock Screen, widgets, car, speakers.

## Supporting elements

- **Now-playing card** with waveform/progress and artwork-tinted background.
- **Mood chips**: Chill · Focus · Workout · Party · Sleep.
- **Lyric line** highlighted in the accent colour (licensed lyrics only).
- **EQ sliders** with presets (Bass, Vocal, Acoustic) as isolated UI pieces.
- **Offline badge**: "Downloaded · {tracks} tracks".
- **Widget / Lock Screen / car display** mocks.
- **Format chips** (true only): FLAC · Lossless · Gapless · Crossfade.

## Visual direction

- Dark, immersive and colourful: midnight or neon bases with gradients extracted from the artwork; brutal/bold condensed type for indie or youth brands.
- Artwork is the hero — large, crisp, and original or properly licensed; generate abstract cover art if you have no rights to real releases.
- Minimal UI chrome; let one colour story run through the whole set.
- Leaders (Spotify, Apple Music, SoundCloud, Poweramp, Musixmatch) all lead with artwork and mood, then personalisation.
- Waveforms, visualisers and subtle motion cues add energy without clutter.
- Avoid headphone stock photography and concert crowds — they say nothing about the app.

## Avoid

- Real artists, album covers, song titles or lyrics without licences — the most common rejection in this niche (Apple 5.2.1, 5.2.3; Google Play IP policy).
- "Free music download", "download from YouTube" — Apple 5.2.3 and Google Play ban downloading from third-party services against their terms.
- "Volume booster 200%" or hearing-risk claims — misleading and potentially harmful.
- Spotify, Apple Music or YouTube logos and names in captions (2.3.7, trademarks).
- Explicit lyrics or cover art in screenshots — must suit all audiences (2.3.8).
- Prices or "free" when offline/ad-free is premium (2.3.7).
- Conversion mistakes: a text list of songs as the hook, small artwork, a settings-heavy EQ shot first, no mood.
