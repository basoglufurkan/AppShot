# Travel Planner

## The searcher

- Planning a trip with 30 browser tabs open — blogs, maps, booking emails, a half-finished spreadsheet — and craving one place for it all.
- The group-trip organiser herding friends or family, collecting votes and splitting costs.
- The spontaneous traveller who lands somewhere and wants "what's good near me, right now?"
- The frequent business traveller who needs every reservation and gate change in one timeline.
- After installing they want to feel: excited, organised, and confident that nothing will go wrong on the day.

## What wins the install

1. **Dream → plan** — inspiration first: a stunning destination with a ready itinerary overlay sells the feeling of the trip.
2. **Everything in one place** — flights, hotels, tickets auto-imported from email kills the tab chaos.
3. **Days on a map** — a visual day-by-day itinerary answers "is this route realistic?"
4. **Instant plans** — AI or template itineraries in minutes for people who hate planning.
5. **Plan together** — shared trips, votes, comments, cost splitting.
6. **Travel-day calm** — offline access, live updates (gate changes, delays), reminders.

## Story (shot by shot)

1. **Hook** — plan your dream trip — show: itinerary over a licensed destination photo — layout: full-bleed — angle: breathtaking place, clean itinerary card floating on it.
2. **Mapped** — your days, mapped out — show: day plan list + map with numbered pins — layout: duo-devices — angle: list on one device, route on the other.
3. **One place** — every booking together — show: flight, hotel and ticket cards — layout: floating-cards — angle: realistic reservation cards (no prices) around the device.
4. **Instant** — an itinerary in minutes — show: AI/plan generator prompt → result — layout: ui-pieces — angle: "4 days in Lisbon with kids" bubble and the generated days.
5. **Together** — plan with your crew — show: shared trip with avatars, votes, comments — layout: hero-device — angle: friends voting on a place.
6. **Travel day** — never miss a gate change — show: live notifications — layout: notification-stack — angle: gate change, check-in open, leave for the airport.
7. **Offline** (optional) — show: offline maps/documents, packing list — layout: feature-list — angle: offline, packing, budget, documents.

## Supporting elements

- **Flight card**: "IST → {city} · Gate {gate} · Boarding {time}" (no fares).
- **Hotel card**: name placeholder, check-in date, confirmation number masked.
- **Map with numbered pins** and walking time between stops.
- **AI prompt bubble**: "4 days in Lisbon, love food, travelling with kids".
- **Notifications**: "Gate changed to {gate}", "Check-in is open", "Leave by {time} to make your flight".
- **Group vote card**: place + avatars + thumbs-up count {votes}.
- **Packing checklist** and **budget split** chips.

## Visual direction

- Inspirational photography is the hero: licensed destination shots, warm sunsets, turquoise coasts (sunset, ocean, sky themes).
- Editorial pairing of a serif display face and a clean sans, like a travel magazine; cards with soft shadows over photos.
- Keep UI readable on busy photos with glass panels or gradient scrims.
- Leaders: Wanderlog (map-first, collaborative), TripIt (timeline, business), Google Travel/Airbnb-style inspiration; combine beauty with one clear utility per shot.
- Vary destinations across shots (beach, city, mountains) to widen appeal; localise to top destinations for each storefront.
- Maps large and simplified — numbered pins beat dense POI clutter.

## Avoid

- Copyrighted photos and protected landmark imagery (e.g. the Eiffel Tower's night illumination is copyrighted) — use licensed or own photos.
- Airline, hotel or OTA logos and brand colours implying partnership (Apple 5.2.1); booking claims if the app only plans.
- Fares, hotel prices or "cheapest flights" claims (2.3.1, 2.3.7).
- Fake reviews or ratings for places; real people's faces without releases.
- Visa or entry-requirement statements presented as authoritative — they change; show them only as an informational feature.
- Conversion mistakes: a generic globe icon, an empty "Create your first trip" state, tiny maps with 40 pins, the same city in every shot.
