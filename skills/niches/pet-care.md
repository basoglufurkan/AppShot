# Pet Care

## The searcher

- Sub-types share this niche — health records & reminders, training, walks/GPS, sitter marketplaces, vet chat; keep the set on the app's real job.
- A new puppy or kitten owner, overwhelmed by vaccines, feeding, training and "am I doing this right?"
- A busy owner who keeps forgetting flea treatment, deworming or medication doses.
- A multi-pet household sharing care across family members.
- After installing they want to feel: a good pet parent — their pet healthy, happy and never missed out on care.

## What wins the install

1. **Love first** — an adorable, real pet front and centre; this niche converts on emotion before features.
2. **Never miss care** — vaccine, medication and treatment reminders kill the guilt of forgetting.
3. **Everything in one place** — vet visits, records, weight, microchip, documents at the vet's desk.
4. **Visible progress** (training) — lessons and milestones that change behaviour.
5. **Activity** (walks) — distance, time and routes for dogs that need exercise.
6. **Help on demand** — vet chat or expert advice, only with licensed professionals.

## Story (shot by shot)

1. **Hook** — everything your pet needs, one app — show: pet profile with photo and today's care — layout: full-bleed — angle: big, joyful pet photo with the profile card floating.

## Supporting elements

- **Pet profile card**: photo, name, breed, age, weight.
- **Notifications**: "Rabies vaccine due in {days} days", "Flea treatment today", "Dinner time for {petName}".
- **Weight chart** with a healthy-range band (as the app defines it).
- **Walk stats**: "{km} km · {minutes} min · {streak}-day streak".
- **Training badge**: "Sit — mastered", "Lesson {n} of {total}".
- **Vet chat bubbles**: owner question → licensed vet's general guidance.
- **Reminder chips**: Vaccine · Deworm · Flea · Grooming · Vet visit.

## Visual direction

- Warm and joyful: playful or pastel palettes, sunset warmth, cream backgrounds; rounded friendly type (Nunito, Baloo, SF Rounded).
- Real pet photography is the hero — expressive faces, natural light, the team's own or licensed pets; match species to the app (dogs, cats or both).
- Paw motifs sparingly; let photos and simple cards do the work.
- Leaders: 11pets and Pawtrack (records), Dogo and Woofz (training, bright and energetic), Rover (marketplace trust), Fi/Tractive (GPS, outdoor).
- Show one pet per shot for clarity; vary breeds across the set to widen appeal.
- Keep medical screens clean and calm — trust over cuteness on health shots.

## Avoid

- Veterinary medical claims: "diagnose your pet", "cure", "replace your vet" — Apple 1.4.1; vet chat must show real licensed professionals and frame advice as general.
- Specific medication doses in screenshots — dosing is weight- and case-specific; risk of harm.
- Branded pet food, medication or toy packaging (trademarks, Apple 5.2.1).
- Pets in distress, unsafe situations or poor welfare (e.g. off-leash near roads).
- Fabricated ratings, "#1 pet app", invented user counts.
- Prices or "free" when reminders/records are premium (2.3.7).
- Conversion mistakes: no pet photo (cold UI), dense medical tables, generic paw icons instead of a real animal, too much text.
