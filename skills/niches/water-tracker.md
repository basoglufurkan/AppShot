# Water Tracker

## The searcher

- Someone who knows they don't drink enough (headaches, tiredness, long screen days) and wants nudges. They search "drink water reminder".
- A fitness or diet app user told to hit a daily target who wants a dedicated, satisfying tracker.
- An office worker or student who simply forgets until evening.
- What they want to feel: "Effortless reminders, and a satisfying feeling as my goal fills up."

This is a low-consideration install. Shots 1–2 do almost all the work, and 5 shots is plenty.

## What wins the install

1. **A satisfying fill-up visual.** A bottle, glass or wave filling toward the goal is delightful and understood instantly.
2. **Reminders, the real job.** Notifications that arrive at sensible times are why people install, so show them early.
3. **One-tap logging and widgets.** Zero friction: tap a cup size, or log from the home screen.
4. **A personal goal.** A target based on weight, activity (and weather, if supported) feels smart, not generic.
5. **Gentle gamification.** Streaks, or a plant or pet that grows as you drink, make the habit stick.

## Story (shot by shot)

1. **Hook** — the satisfying goal — show: a big filling bottle "{intake} / {goal} ml" — layout: big-number — angle: drink enough, effortlessly.
2. **Reminders** — the core job — show: a stack of friendly reminder notifications through the day — layout: notification-stack — angle: never forget to drink.
3. **One tap** — zero friction — show: cup size buttons and a home-screen widget pulled out of the UI — layout: ui-pieces — angle: one tap per glass.
4. **Personal goal** — smart — show: goal card based on weight and activity — layout: floating-cards — angle: a goal sized for you.
5. **Habit** — momentum — show: weekly chart, streak, or the growing plant/pet — layout: hero-device — angle: little sips, big habit.

## Supporting elements

- Filling bottle or wave progress: "{intake} ml of {goal} ml", with percent.
- Notification: "Time for a glass of water", in the app's real copy and tone.
- Cup size chips: 200 ml, 330 ml, 500 ml. Use the app's real presets, and oz for US screenshots.
- Home-screen widget mockup showing today's progress.
- Streak chip: "{streak}-day streak". Weekly bar chart with the goal line.
- Goal card: Weight {weight} · Activity {activity} → "{goal} ml / day".
- Gamified companion: plant or pet at its current growth stage (only if the app has one).

## Visual direction

- Sky and ocean blues, aqua gradients, droplets and wave shapes. Use playful when the app has a character or plant, and mint for a fresh, clean look.
- Typography: rounded friendly sans with big numerals and short captions.
- Simple, bright, uncluttered layouts. One idea per shot.
- Leaders use cute characters or plants, a big bottle fill, and notification stacks. Clinical medical styling makes a light habit app feel heavy.

## Avoid

- Health claims: "detox", "flush toxins", "clear skin", "lose weight by drinking water", "cures headaches". These are misleading under Apple 1.4.1 and Google Play's health misinformation policy.
- Extreme intake goals in demo data (overhydration is a real risk). Don't recommend intake for kidney or heart conditions.
- Caffeine or alcohol "counting toward hydration" claims without a source.
- Fake streaks, invented "{users} people hydrated today" counts, or "#1 water app".
- Prices, "remove ads" upsells or trial text in screenshots.
- Conversion killers: five shots of the same ring, a clinical tone, and no reminder shot (it's the core job).
