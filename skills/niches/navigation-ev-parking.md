# Navigation, EV Charging & Parking

## The searcher

- Three jobs share this niche — identify which one the app serves and keep the whole set on it:
- **Navigation**: the commuter who wants to beat traffic, the trucker or motorcyclist with special routing needs, the traveller without data who needs offline maps.
- **EV charging**: the electric-car driver with range anxiety, wanting a working, compatible, available charger — and a trip plan with charging stops.
- **Parking**: the city driver circling the block, hunting for a spot and dreading tickets, wanting to pay and extend from the phone.
- Often searched in the moment (in or near the car); after installing they want to feel: calm, on time, no surprises.

## What wins the install

1. **The outcome of the sub-job** — "arrive on time", "charge without guessing", "park and pay in seconds" — pick one hook.
2. **Live, trustworthy data** — real-time traffic, charger availability, parking spaces: freshness is the product.
3. **Compatibility & filters** (EV) — plug type, power, network; kills "will it work with my car?"
4. **In-car experience** — CarPlay / Android Auto support, big readable guidance.
5. **Remote control** (parking) — extend a session from the café, get reminded before it expires.
6. **Safety and clarity** — speed limits, lane guidance, offline maps; legal speed-camera alerts only where allowed.

## Story (shot by shot)

1. **Hook** — the sub-job outcome — show: route with ETA / charger found / parking paid — layout: tilted-device — angle: 3D map with route line in brand colour and one key card.
2. **Live** — smarter routes, calmer drives — show: live traffic with a faster route suggestion — layout: device-top — angle: crop to the map and the "saves {minutes} min" chip.
3. **Find** — the right charger or spot, fast — show: charger/parking detail with availability and connector types — layout: floating-cards — angle: availability, power and plug chips around the device.
4. **Plan** — long trips with charging stops — show: route plan with stops and arrival charge — layout: zoom-callout — angle: zoom on the stop list with arrival %.
5. **Remind** — never get a ticket — show: notifications (parking expiring, charge complete, leave now) — layout: notification-stack — angle: actionable alert with "Extend".
6. **In the car** — on your dashboard — show: CarPlay / Android Auto screen (if supported) — layout: hero-device — angle: car display mock, no hand-held phone.
7. **Extras** (optional) — show: offline maps, lane guidance, speed limits — layout: feature-list — angle: 4 short proof points.

## Supporting elements

- **ETA card**: "{eta} min · {distance} · arrive {time}".
- **Reroute notification**: "Faster route found — saves {minutes} min" (only real suggestions).
- **Charger card**: "{available}/{total} available · CCS · {kw} kW" with network name as text.
- **Range ring**: "Arrive with {percent}%".
- **Parking session timer**: "Expires {time}" + Extend button.
- **Notifications**: "Parking ends in 10 min", "Charging complete", "Leave now to arrive on time".
- **Speed-limit sign** in the correct national design for the storefront.

## Visual direction

- Map-centric: dark maps for driving and night (midnight, ocean), bright route line in brand colour; EV apps lean volt green, parking apps confident blue.
- Tilted 3D map views and big guidance arrows convey motion; keep cards large for at-a-glance reading.
- Leaders: Google Maps (clean), Waze (playful, community), ABRP (data-rich EV planning), PlugShare (community chargers), ParkMobile/EasyPark (simple sessions).
- In-car shots use a licensed car-interior photo with the CarPlay/Android Auto display; never a phone held while driving.
- Keep map attribution (© OpenStreetMap, provider names) visible where required.
- Localise: city, road signs, units (km vs mi) and charging networks per storefront.

## Avoid

- Distracted driving: no hands holding phones while driving, no typing while moving — Apple 1.4.5 forbids urging risky behaviour; show mounted phones or CarPlay.
- Speed-camera alerts are restricted or illegal in some countries (e.g. Germany, Switzerland, France) — localise or drop; never "avoid the police".
- CarPlay / Android Auto names and imagery only if supported, per Apple and Google guidelines.
- Charging-network, car-maker or parking-operator logos implying partnership (Apple 5.2.1).
- Fuel, charging or parking prices in screenshots (2.3.7); "free parking" claims.
- Removing or hiding map data attribution.
- Conversion mistakes: a generic city map with no story, mixing all three jobs in one set, tiny guidance text, dark maps with dark captions.
