# Collage & Story Maker

## The searcher

- Someone who wants stories and posts that look designed, with no design skills or desktop tools.
- A person with a moment to share (birthday, trip, graduation, a monthly photo dump) who needs a template that does it justice tonight.
- A small business or creator posting promos who wants on-brand looks without a designer.
- What they want to feel: "My story looks like a brand made it, and it took me two minutes."

## What wins the install

1. **Template quality and volume.** A fan of beautiful finished designs sells immediately. People install for the templates, not the editor.
2. **Your photos in, design out.** Templates filled with real-looking personal photos make it feel attainable.
3. **Occasions.** Birthday, travel, love, sale. Searchers arrive with a specific occasion, so naming it converts.
4. **Easy customization.** Drag and drop, fonts and stickers show it's "yours", not a cookie-cutter.
5. **Seamless carousels.** A trend that the bridge layout can literally demonstrate across shots.
6. **Right size, every platform.** Story, post and square exports remove friction.

## Supporting elements

- Template category chips: Birthday, Travel, Love, Minimal, Sale, Photo dump.
- Layout picker icons: 2×2, 3×3, filmstrip, freeform.
- Font sample card: three real font styles from the app, each rendering the word "Summer".
- Sticker sheet cutout and a tape-and-paper texture strip.
- Size chips: Story 9:16, Post 4:5, Square 1:1.
- Badge: "{templates} templates", using the real count available to the viewer.
- Drag indicator: a photo mid-drop into a frame, with a soft shadow.

## Visual direction

- Pastel, cream and playful palettes with scrapbook textures, paper, tape and soft shadows. Use editorial for a minimalist "aesthetic" positioning.
- Show finished designs large. Editor chrome appears only in the customization shot.
- Fanned and duo devices give the set a rich, abundant feel. Keep backgrounds calm so the templates pop.
- Typography: a trendy serif plus script pairing that itself demonstrates style range.
- Use diverse, licensed lifestyle photos (friends, travel, food, pets) and keep the color grading consistent between templates.
- Leading apps run editorial minimal templates, neutral beige palettes and fans of story devices. Garish clip-art collages look dated.

## Avoid

- Instagram, TikTok or Facebook logos, icons and UI chrome. Use generic story frames (Apple 5.2.1, Meta brand terms, Play impersonation).
- Template photos, fonts or illustrations you don't have commercial rights to showcase (Apple 2.3.9).
- Template counts that include locked or paid content without that being clear in-app. No prices or "premium" price tags.
- Year-specific or holiday-specific designs as hero shots. They date the listing quickly and Play discourages time-sensitive text.
- No "#1 story app" or invented usage figures.
- Conversion killers: an empty editor as the hook, too much UI chrome, tiny templates in a grid nobody can see, and inconsistent template quality.
