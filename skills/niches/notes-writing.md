# Notes & Writing

## The searcher

- A student taking lecture notes, often on an iPad with a stylus, who wants handwriting that's tidy, searchable and synced.
- A writer (blogger, novelist, screenwriter) who wants a distraction-free, beautiful place for words.
- A knowledge worker building a "second brain" — linking ideas, meeting notes, research.
- A meeting-heavy professional who wants to record, transcribe and get an AI summary instead of typing.
- After installing they want to feel: ideas are safe, thinking is clearer, nothing gets lost.

## What wins the install

1. **Capture before it's gone** — opening to a blank note instantly, widgets, voice capture; speed is the first test.
2. **Find anything** — search across text, handwriting, images and PDFs; tags and links.
3. **A writing space that feels good** — typography, focus mode, dark/sepia; writers choose with their eyes.
4. **Handwriting on tablet** — realistic ink, paper templates, PDF annotation (students).
5. **AI that saves time** — voice → structured note, long meeting → summary and action items.
6. **Sync, ownership & privacy** — every device, offline, export/plain files, encryption where true.

## Story (shot by shot)

1. **Hook** — your ideas, beautifully kept — show: one gorgeous note (writing or handwriting) — layout: hero-device — angle: real content, elegant type, nothing else on screen.
2. **Capture** — get it down in seconds — show: quick note widget, voice capture, share-sheet clip — layout: ui-pieces — angle: three entry points lifted out of the UI.
3. **Connect** — every thought, linked — show: folders/tags + backlinks or graph — layout: duo-devices — angle: structure on one device, connections on the other.
4. **Find** — search everything — show: search hitting handwriting or text inside an image — layout: zoom-callout — angle: zoom on the highlighted match.
5. **Summarise** — from messy to clear — show: raw transcript → AI summary with action items — layout: before-after — angle: wall of text vs 4 clean bullets.
6. **Everywhere** — phone, tablet, desktop in sync — show: the same note on multiple screens — layout: device-top — angle: large tablet view cropped, phone overlapping.
7. **Private** (optional) — your notes are yours — show: lock/encryption/export settings — layout: feature-list — angle: offline, export, Face ID, encryption — only what's true.

## Supporting elements

- **AI summary card**: "Summary · 4 key points · 3 action items" from a fictional meeting.
- **Voice chip**: waveform + "{minutes} min recording → note".
- **Backlink graph** fragment: 5–7 nodes, one highlighted.
- **Search highlight** over handwriting or a photo of a whiteboard.
- **Tag chips**: #ideas · #lecture · #project.
- **Sync status**: "Synced to iPad · just now".
- **Privacy badge** (true only): "End-to-end encrypted", "Stored locally", "Export to Markdown".
- **Word count / streak** for writing apps.

## Visual direction

- Typography-led: cream/paper and editorial themes with a refined serif for writing apps (iA Writer, Bear, Ulysses style); mono accents for Markdown/developer audiences.
- Handwriting apps (GoodNotes, Notability) show realistic coloured ink on paper templates, large on an iPad — the tablet set matters most.
- Knowledge apps (Obsidian, Notion, Craft) can go midnight with a glowing graph, but keep text legible.
- Use real, well-written sample content (your own essay, lecture, meeting) — never lorem ipsum.
- One note per shot; crop with device-top or zoom-callout so text stays readable in thumbnails.
- Quiet backgrounds, minimal decoration; the writing is the visual.

## Avoid

- Encryption, privacy or "never lose a note" claims that aren't strictly true (Apple 2.3.1).
- AI summaries with hallucinated or wrong content — readers check.
- Apple Pencil / iPad naming outside Apple's marketing guidelines; no competitor names in captions ("GoodNotes alternative") (2.3.7).
- Copyrighted lecture slides, textbook pages or other people's writing in sample notes; real personal data in "meeting notes".
- Lorem ipsum or "Test note 1" — looks unfinished and risks rejection under Apple 2.1/2.3.3.
- Conversion mistakes: an empty note as the hook, tiny body text, toolbar-heavy screenshots, a folder list as shot 1.
