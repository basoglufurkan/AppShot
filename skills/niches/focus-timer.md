# Focus Timer & App Blocker

## The searcher

- A student before exams who opens the books and ends up on social media for an hour — frustrated with themselves.
- A remote worker losing whole afternoons to the phone and notifications.
- Someone who just saw their weekly screen-time report and felt a jolt of shame.
- People who need external structure to start (procrastinators, people with attention struggles — keep it non-medical).
- After installing they want to feel: back in control of their attention, proud of focused hours, calmer.

## What wins the install

1. **Block the scroll** — real app/website blocking during sessions is the actual job; a timer alone is a commodity.
2. **Focus made visible** — streaks, focused hours, a growing tree/plant: progress you can feel.
3. **Start in one tap** — no setup friction; the timer runs immediately.
4. **Atmosphere** — ambient sounds and lofi make the session something to look forward to.
5. **Accountability** — friends, focus rooms, study-with-me sessions.
6. **Insight** — when and where you focus best, without judgement.

## Story (shot by shot)

1. **Hook** — get your focus back — show: running focus session with big timer — layout: full-bleed — angle: calm ambient scene, timer huge, phone feels quiet.
2. **Block** — distracting apps, paused — show: blocked-apps screen during a session — layout: before-after — angle: notification chaos vs calm session (generic app silhouettes, no real logos).
3. **Proof** — see your focused hours grow — show: weekly stats — layout: big-number — angle: "{hours} h focused this week" from real data.
4. **Reward** — every session counts — show: streak/garden/badges — layout: floating-cards — angle: grown items or badges around the device.
5. **Together** — focus with friends — show: focus room with avatars — layout: hero-device — angle: live session with people's statuses.
6. **Glance** — timer on the Lock Screen — show: Live Activity / widget and break notification — layout: notification-stack — angle: "Session complete — take 5".
7. **Sounds** (optional) — find your flow — show: sound picker — layout: feature-list — angle: rain, café, lofi, brown noise.

## Visual direction

- Calm and grounded: deep greens (forest), soft mint, warm sunset for lofi/cozy apps, midnight for night study.
- Huge tabular numerals for the timer; minimal surrounding UI so the shot itself feels focused.
- Full-bleed ambient scenes (rain on a window, a quiet desk, a forest) work well behind the device.
- Leaders (Forest, Opal, one sec, Focus To-Do, Flora) use a companion metaphor (tree, gem, plant) — show it if the app has one.
- Avoid harsh red "blocked" warnings; frame blocking as protection, not punishment.
- Keep captions high contrast — dark calm themes easily drift into unreadable grey-on-grey.

## Avoid

- Real third-party app icons (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube) shown as "blocked" — trademark issues on both stores (Apple 5.2.1, Google Play IP); use generic silhouettes or category names.
- Medical claims: "treats ADHD", "cures phone addiction", "boosts IQ" — Apple 1.4.1, Google Play health-claims rules.
- Fabricated before/after screen-time numbers.
- Promising blocking the platform can't deliver: iOS blocking relies on Apple's Screen Time / Family Controls APIs; Android uses different mechanisms — keep each store's screenshots platform-true (Apple 2.3.1).
- Prices or "free" when blocking is premium-only (2.3.7).
- Conversion mistakes: a bare timer as the hook (every competitor has one), low-contrast dark captions, guilt-tripping copy ("You wasted 5 hours").
