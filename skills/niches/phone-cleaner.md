# Phone & Storage Cleaner

## The searcher

- The person who just got "Storage Almost Full" and can't take a photo, record a video or install an update — urgent and irritated.
- Someone with tens of thousands of photos (duplicates, bursts, screenshots) who's overwhelmed and doesn't know where to start.
- A user avoiding a bigger cloud-storage plan or a new phone.
- Android users wanting to clear junk files and cache; iPhone users wanting photo, video and contact cleanup.
- After installing they want to feel: relief, space back, a tidy gallery — and certainty that no precious memory was lost.

## What wins the install

1. **Space back, measured** — the outcome is gigabytes; a big, honest number (from a real demo device) is the hook.
2. **Safe by design** — review before deleting, keep-the-best suggestions, recoverable deletions: the core objection is "will I lose my memories?"
3. **Smart grouping** — duplicates, similar shots, blurry photos, screenshots, large videos found automatically.
4. **Satisfying swipe** — keep/delete swiping turns a dreaded chore into something oddly fun.
5. **Compress, don't delete** — shrink videos and photos while keeping them.
6. **Contacts and more** — merge duplicate contacts, clean old calendar events.

## Visual direction

- Fresh and light: white, sky and mint with glassy cards; green for progress and "space freed", never red alarms.
- Big numerals for GB and counts; ring charts and clean grids.
- Use pleasant, licensed or team-shot photos (landscapes, food, pets) in grids — no strangers' faces, nothing intimate.
- Leaders (Cleanup, Slidebox, Smart Cleaner, Clever Cleaner) win with a strong "before" mess and a satisfying "after" order.
- Make the swipe card large and tactile; it's the most memorable interaction.
- Keep the iOS/Android sets platform-true in features and visuals.

## Avoid

- Fake alerts and scare UI: "Your iPhone is infected", "Battery damaged", fake scans with invented junk counts, mimicked iOS system dialogs — rejected under Apple 2.3.1 and banned by Google Play's deceptive-behaviour policy.
- Impossible claims on iOS: "boost speed", "clear RAM", "cool down your phone", "clean other apps' cache", "antivirus" — the sandbox doesn't allow it, and Apple rejects it.
- Google Play: "speed booster"/"battery saver" claims that don't do what they say are treated as misleading functionality.
- Invented GB numbers; any figure must come from a real cleanup on the demo device and be labelled as such.
- Apple's iCloud logo, Settings UI or device imagery used to imply an official tool (5.2.1).
- Real people's faces without releases, private or intimate photos in grids.
- Prices or "free" when deletion is paywalled (2.3.7).
- Conversion mistakes: abstract icons instead of photos, red danger palettes, five features in one shot.
