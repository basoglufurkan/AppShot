# Caption anti-patterns

Thirty-three mistakes that sink screenshot captions, each with the reason it fails and a rewrite. Use this as a lint pass after drafting; `rewrite-drills.md` is the full procedure.

## Content

### 1. Feature-speak
Naming the mechanism instead of what the user gets.
- ✗ "Advanced expense categorisation" → ✓ "See where every **dollar** goes"

### 2. Internal jargon and code names
Users have never heard of your sync engine.
- ✗ "Sync Engine 2.0" → ✓ "Your notes on every **device**"
- ✗ "Smart Ledger™ technology" → ✓ "Balances that update **themselves**"

### 3. Vague superlatives
"Best", "ultimate" and "amazing" carry no information and invite legal scrutiny.
- ✗ "The best budgeting app ever" → ✓ "A budget you'll actually **keep**"

### 4. Generic stock benefits
They could sit on any app in any category.
- ✗ "Save time and money" → ✓ "Groceries for **less**, planned in minutes"

### 5. Several ideas in one headline
At thumbnail size, a list is noise.
- ✗ "Track, plan, share and save" → ✓ "Plan your week in **minutes**" (the other ideas get their own shots)

### 6. Noun lists with no benefit
- ✗ "Budgets. Goals. Reports." → ✓ "Budgets that **build** themselves"

### 7. Describing the screenshot
The viewer can see that it's a dashboard.
- ✗ "This is the dashboard" / "Home screen" → ✓ "Your whole month at a **glance**"

### 8. Caption–screen mismatch
The claim must be visible on the device below it.
- ✗ "Plan meals for the week" over a settings screen → ✓ the same caption over the weekly planner, or a caption that matches the screen shown

### 9. AI as the benefit
"AI-powered" is a mechanism, not an outcome.
- ✗ "AI-powered budgeting" → ✓ "Budgets that **adjust** themselves" (subhead: "Learns from your spending, on device")

### 10. Hedging
"Can", "may" and "helps you" water the promise down. Replace it with a true, concrete mechanism.
- ✗ "Can help you possibly sleep better" → ✓ "Drift off to **calm** sounds"

### 11. Weak verbs
Manage, handle, use, get, do.
- ✗ "Manage your tasks" → ✓ "Finish what **matters**"

## Truth and policy

### 12. Invented numbers
- ✗ "Join 1M+ happy users" → ✓ "Join **{users}** savers" (verified), with fallback "No ads, no **data** selling" (verified)

### 13. Fear-mongering
Scaring people about health or money reads as manipulative and triggers review flags.
- ✗ "Your bad sleep is killing you" → ✓ "Wake up **rested**"

### 14. Fake urgency and CTAs
"Download now" sits next to the Get button anyway, and Play discourages CTAs in screenshots.
- ✗ "Download now!" → ✓ the benefit itself: "A clear budget by **Sunday**"

### 15. Competitor and platform references
These are trademark and policy problems (Apple 2.3.10, Play metadata policy).
- ✗ "Better than MyFitnessPal" / "Also on Android" → ✓ "Log meals from **one photo**"

### 16. Fine print in the headline
- ✗ "7-day free trial, then $59.99/year" as the headline → ✓ headline: "Coaching that **adapts**"; trial and price terms go on the paywall, not in any screenshot text (Apple 2.3.7, Play graphics rules)

### 17. Time-sensitive copy in the evergreen set
- ✗ "New for 2024!" / "Black Friday deal" → ✓ an evergreen benefit. Seasonal hooks go in custom product pages.

## Language and translation

### 18. Untranslatable puns
They die in 20 locales and confuse non-native English readers too.
- ✗ "Lettuce help you eat better" → ✓ "Eat **better**, skip the guesswork"

### 19. Literal idiom translation
- ✗ EN "Hit the ground running" → DE "Den Boden laufend treffen" → ✓ "Sofort **loslegen**"

### 20. Inconsistent person or voice
Mixing you/we/users, or du/Sie, across shots.
- ✗ "Track your runs" / "We plan your meals" / "Users can share" → ✓ second person throughout
- ✗ "Plane deine Woche" / "Legen Sie Ihr Ziel fest" → ✓ "Plane deine Woche" / "Leg dein **Ziel** fest"

### 21. Exclusionary assumptions
- ✗ "For busy moms" (an app for all parents) → ✓ "For busy **parents**"
- ✗ "Bro, track your gains" → ✓ "Track every **lift**"

### 22. Gendered address in gendered languages
- ✗ FR "Prêt ?" / ES "¿Listo?" / PL "Gotowy?" → ✓ "On y va ?" / "¿Empezamos?" / "Zaczynamy?"

## Form

### 23. Too long
Sentences don't read at thumbnail size (limits are in `length.md`).
- ✗ "Easily track all of your daily expenses and income in one simple place" → ✓ "Every expense, one **place**"

### 24. ALL CAPS
It's harder to read, it shouts, and it breaks German ß and stacked Vietnamese or Thai marks.
- ✗ "TRACK YOUR WORKOUTS" → ✓ "Track every **workout**"

### 25. Over-highlighting
More than two accent words means no accent.
- ✗ "**Track** your **daily** **expenses**" → ✓ "Track **every** expense"

### 26. Highlighting the wrong word
Pronouns, articles, the brand, or half a word.
- ✗ "Track **your** expenses" → ✓ "Track **every** expense"
- In agglutinative languages, highlight the whole word including suffixes; never leave a suffix outside the highlight.

### 27. Brand name in every headline
The name already sits beside the icon.
- ✗ "Budgetly tracks spending" / "Budgetly sets goals" → ✓ "Spending, **tracked**" / "Goals you can **see**"

### 28. Repeated words across shots
- ✗ "Easy budgets" / "Easy goals" / "Easy reports" → ✓ "Budgets in **minutes**" / "Goals you can **see**" / "Reports that **explain**"

### 29. Same structure every shot
- ✗ "Easily plan…" / "Easily track…" / "Easily share…" → ✓ mix formulas: nominal, imperative, contrast (see `sequence.md`)

### 30. A subhead that repeats the headline
- ✗ H "Track your spending" · S "Easily track all your spending" → ✓ H "Spending, **tracked**" · S "Sorted into categories from your bank feed"

### 31. Obvious or insulting questions
- ✗ "Want to save money?" / "Tired of being broke?" → ✓ "Where did your **money** go?"

### 32. Exclamation and emoji overload
- ✗ "Super easy!!! 🎉🔥" → ✓ "Set up in **one minute**" (if true)

### 33. Broken line breaks
Orphans, split units, a highlight broken across lines.
- ✗ "Save money on every / purchase you / make" → ✓ "Save on every / **purchase**"
- ✗ "Ready in 5 / minutes" → ✓ "Ready in / **5 minutes**" (keep the number and unit together)

## Machine-checkable lint

Before rendering, flag any caption that:
- has more than 6 words in the headline or 12 in the subhead
- has more than 2 `**…**` pairs, or unbalanced markers
- contains an ALL-CAPS word longer than 3 letters (except real acronyms such as PDF or SAT)
- contains more than one "!" across the set, or any emoji not used in the app UI
- contains an unfilled `{placeholder}` at export time
- contains a word from the overused lists in `power-words.md`
- repeats a content word used in another headline of the same set and locale (compare roots, not inflected forms)
- mentions Android, Google Play or iPhone on the other store's listing, a competitor brand, or a four-digit year
- contains "Download", "Install" or "Get it now"
