# Power words and benefit verbs (EN + TR)

Power words don't convert by themselves. They sharpen a specific benefit. Use these lists to replace a weak verb or a vague adjective in a draft, never to decorate one. **One power word per headline;** the rest should be concrete nouns from the app's world.

Turkish verbs are given in the *sen* imperative. For *siz*, add -in/-ın/-un/-ün (bitir → bitirin, sakla → saklayın). Turkish equivalents are natural choices, not dictionary translations, so pick the one that matches the app's UI vocabulary.

## Speed

| EN verb | TR verb | EN power words | TR power words |
|---|---|---|---|
| finish | bitir | instantly | anında |
| skip | atla | one tap | tek dokunuş(la) |
| get done | hallet | real-time | anlık, canlı |
| jump straight to | doğrudan geç | on the spot | o an, yerinde |
| speed up | hızlandır | no waiting | beklemeden |
| knock out (tasks) | çabucak bitir | shortcut | kısayol |
| — | — | same-day | aynı gün |

- **Use for:** utilities, scanning, invoicing, editing, delivery, planning.
- **Watch out:** "in seconds" / "saniyeler içinde" is on every listing; prefer a concrete unit (one photo, one sentence). In Turkish CTAs, "hemen" ("Hemen indir") sounds pushy.

## Turkish word-building tools

These are short, idiomatic and very effective in headlines:
- **-sız/-siz/-suz/-süz (without):** reklamsız, zahmetsiz, sınırsız, internetsiz, dertsiz, ezbersiz.
- **-lı/-li (with):** hatırlatmalı, sesli, grafikli. Useful in subheads.
- **tek (one):** tek dokunuş, tek ekran, tek liste, tek yer.
- **kendi (own):** kendi temponda, kendi planın.
- **sana özel:** the short way to say "personalised".
- **cebinde (in your pocket)**, and **artık (now / no more)** for freshness: "Artık unutma".
- **Reduplication for emphasis:** adım adım, gün gün, kuruşu kuruşuna, tıkır tıkır (running smoothly).

## Overused words to avoid

They say nothing specific, appear on every competing listing, and some ("best", "#1") create legal exposure. Replace each with the concrete outcome, object or number it is hiding.

**English:** seamless, revolutionary, ultimate, powerful, innovative, next-level, game-changer, all-in-one (unless naming the things), best, amazing, awesome, magical, intuitive, user-friendly, easy-to-use, smart (as filler), unlock, elevate, supercharge, empower, journey, experience, solution, leverage, robust, cutting-edge, world-class, simply, effortlessly (more than once per set).

**Türkçe:** muhteşem, harika, inanılmaz, mükemmel, kusursuz, en iyi, eşsiz, benzersiz, devrim niteliğinde, yeni nesil, son teknoloji, sorunsuz, kullanıcı dostu, deneyim (as filler), çözüm, akıllı (as filler), sihirli, kolay ve hızlı, hayatını değiştir, hemen (in CTAs), keşfet (on every shot; every Turkish listing says it).

**Replacement examples:**
- "Powerful photo editor" → "Remove objects in **one tap**"
- "Muhteşem grafikler" → "Harcamaların **renk renk** ayrılsın"
- "Akıllı bütçe" → "Kendini **ayarlayan** bütçe"
- "Seamless sync" → "Start on iPhone, finish on **iPad**"
