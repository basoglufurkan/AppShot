# Completing brief.md

`node scripts/brief.mjs` writes the facts. You turn them into a positioning a stranger understands in five seconds.
Every caption must trace to a line here; if a caption cannot, it is decoration — cut it.

## Positioning line

`For <who>, <app> <does what> so they <outcome>.` One sentence, no adjectives that could describe any app.

- Weak: "For everyone, Pulse is a powerful fitness app so they can be healthy."
- Strong: "For busy people who skip the gym, Pulse builds a 25-minute home workout each morning so they get stronger
  without equipment or planning."

## Searcher and moment

Who types the query, when, and what they feel right before. Take the niche file's "The searcher" and narrow it to this
app's real audience (onboarding quiz options and paywall copy reveal it).

## The objection to kill

The reason a qualified searcher would *not* install: "I don't have time", "it's another app I'll abandon", "AI apps
are gimmicks", "my data isn't private". One shot in the set must answer it with a real screen.

## Benefits ranked

Three to six benefits in user words, each with the screen that proves it and the shot number it goes to.
Rank by: how much the searcher cares × how visible it is in a screen × how different it is from competitors.
Capabilities from `profile.features` are evidence, not benefits: "HealthKit" → "Your workouts land in Apple Health".

## Proof

Only real, sourced items (value, source, date). Empty is fine — most great sets have no ratings at all.

## Keywords

Primary 2–3 (shots 1–3), secondary 3–5 (later shots, subheads). Per locale, chosen per `skills/aso/keywords.md`.

## Voice

Infer from UI strings: playful (emoji, exclamation, nicknames), premium (short, calm, no emoji), clinical (precise,
measured), friendly (you, simple words), bold (imperatives, short). See `skills/copy/voice.md`.

## Visual direction

Theme candidates (niche frontmatter + app look), brand accent, dark vs light, panorama yes/no, device treatment.
Note how the top three competitors look in search so the set stands out instead of blending in.
