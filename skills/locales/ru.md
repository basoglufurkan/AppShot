# Russian (Русский)

The Russian listing reaches far beyond Russia: Kazakhstan, Belarus, Kyrgyzstan, Uzbekistan, the Baltics, Israel and the diaspora all read it. Keep copy free of country-specific assumptions and politics.

**Commerce note:** App Store and Google Play billing for users in Russia has been heavily restricted since 2022. Check current payment availability before building a paywall-led narrative for that storefront. Other Russian-reading markets are unaffected.

## Voice and register

- **"вы" is the default** for most app marketing: finance, health, productivity, education, utilities. It is written **lowercase** in mass communication; capitalised "Вы" is for personal letters and looks odd in a headline.
- **"ты"** fits games, social, youth and lifestyle fitness brands whose UI uses ты.
- **Avoid the choice:** impersonal and nominal headlines are very natural in Russian.
  - "Все расходы под контролем"
  - "Крепкий сон без таблеток"
  - "Английский — каждый день"
- **Tone:** confident, concrete, a little dry. Russian readers are sceptical of hype and of "too good to be true". Specific numbers (real ones) and clear mechanisms persuade.
- **Imperatives** ("Начните", "Следите", "Экономьте") are fine; don't stack them across every shot.

## Typography rules

- **Quotes:** «ёлочки» primary, „лапки“ nested. Never "…".
- **Dash:** the em dash with spaces (" — ") is the standard тире in headlines like "Финансы — по полочкам". Use a non-breaking space before the dash so it never starts a line. The hyphen (-) is only for compounds.
- **Ё:** in general text it is often written as е, but use **ё** where meaning depends on it.
  - "Всё под контролем" = everything is under control; "Все" = all of them / everyone.
  - Also "ещё", "счёт", "учёт". Check the font has ё.
- **Sentence case only;** no capital after a colon.
- **Numbers:** space as the thousands separator ("10 000"), comma decimal ("4,5"), "499 ₽" with a space, and a non-breaking space between number and unit. Dates "4 ноября"; times "21:30".
- **Line breaks:** don't end a line with a one-letter preposition or conjunction (в, к, с, и, а, о, у); bind it to the next word with an NBSP.

## Length

- Expect **+15–30% characters** versus English; long participles and adjectives ("персонализированный", "автоматически") eat lines.
- Headline target ≤ 30 characters.
- Shorteners:
  - "персонализированный план" → "план под вас"
  - "в режиме реального времени" → "сразу"/"онлайн"
  - "с помощью приложения" → delete
  - nominal "X — Y" constructions

## Store and search habits

- **Queries:**
  - money: "учёт расходов", "бюджет", "финансы"
  - health and fitness: "счётчик калорий", "тренировки дома", "шагомер", "медитация", "сон"
  - learning and everyday: "английский язык", "рецепты", "заметки", "погода"
  - creative and other: "фоторедактор", "VPN"
- Users frequently type "е" for "ё", so don't rely on ё-only keywords.
- **Russian Android alternatives exist** (RuStore), but Play and the App Store remain the listing sources for most Russian readers outside Russia.
- **Trust levers:** offline use, no ads, no account required, and data privacy claims backed by the code.
- **Local reference style:** Yandex, VK and T-Bank set the bar with clean, practical captions, nominal headlines and restrained colour.

## Pitfalls

- **Capitalised "Вы"**, missing ё, straight quotes, hyphens used as dashes, and calques ("это делает смысл", "имейте контроль").
- **Desktop verbs on mobile:** "кликните" → "нажмите". "Скачайте сейчас!" is pushy and redundant on a store page.
- **Federal Law "On Advertising" (38-ФЗ)** requires substantiation for "лучший", "№ 1" and "самый" claims. "Бесплатно" must be accurate. Recent language-protection rules push consumer-facing text towards Russian equivalents over English loanwords, so prefer Russian terms.
- **Political symbols, maps and assumptions about country.** The listing is read across many states.
- **Ukrainian or Kazakh audiences have their own locales.** Don't present Russian as the "local" language for them.
