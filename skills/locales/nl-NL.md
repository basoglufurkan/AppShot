# Dutch (Nederlands)

Dutch readers are direct, sceptical of exaggeration and fluent in English. They will happily install an English app, so the Dutch listing has to feel *more* useful than the English default, not merely translated.

## Voice and register

- **"je/jij" is standard** in Dutch consumer marketing, including most banks and telcos.
- **"u"** remains for government, healthcare, insurance, legal and some older-audience services. Follow the app's Dutch strings.
- **No-nonsense tone:** "Doe maar normaal" culture punishes hype. "Gewoon goed" persuades more than "Fantastisch!". Concrete outcomes and plain speech win.
- **Dry humour** works. Earnest superlatives don't.
- **Imperatives** ("Regel", "Bespaar", "Plan") and short noun phrases ("Je geldzaken op orde") are both natural.

## Typography rules

- **Sentence case.** Nouns are lowercase (unlike German).
- **IJ is one letter at a capital position:** "IJsland", "IJzersterk", never "Ijs".
- **Diaeresis and accents** must render: ideeën, privé, één (emphatic "one"), cafeïne.
- **Quotes:** ‘ ’ or “ ” (both common); choose one.
- **Numbers:** "€ 4,99" (euro sign before the amount, with a space: the Dutch convention), comma decimal, thousands with a point ("1.000"). Times "21:30"; dates "4 november" (months lowercase).
- **Compound words** are long ("boodschappenlijstje", "huishoudboekje"). Never break them with a visible hyphen in a headline; use a soft hyphen (U+00AD) at a compound seam only if unavoidable.
- **Line breaks:** don't strand "de/het/een/en" at line end.

## Length

- Expect **+10–25% characters** versus English.
- Headline target ≤ 30 characters.
- Shorten with:
  - noun phrases ("Alles op één plek")
  - "zonder + noun" ("zonder gedoe", "zonder reclame")
  - the shorter of two synonyms ("snel" over "razendsnel")
- English loanwords (app, workout, tracker, budget) are accepted and often shorter.

## Store and search habits

- **Queries:**
  - money: "huishoudboekje", "budget app", "geld besparen"
  - fitness and health: "calorieën tellen", "hardlopen", "workout", "meditatie"
  - home and everyday: "boodschappenlijst", "recepten", "weer", "OV" (public transport), "pakket volgen"
- **Mixed-language search is common.** Many Dutch users search in English, so captions can echo an English category word if the app's UI uses it.
- **Payments:**
  - **iDEAL** is the national payment method.
  - **Tikkie** is so common that "een tikkie sturen" means "request money". It is a trademark, so use it only if integrated.
  - Users are cost-conscious: "gratis", "zonder abonnement" and "geen reclame" are strong, honest levers.
- **Belgium (Flanders) also reads nl-NL** listings. Avoid strongly Hollandic slang ("lekker bezig" is fine; Randstad slang is not).
- **Local reference style:** Dutch market leaders (Buienradar, bol, Albert Heijn, Bunq) use plain, useful, slightly playful captions with no hype and lots of white space.

## Transcreation examples

| English | Nederlands | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Track every expense | Elke **uitgave** in beeld | "in beeld" = in view |
| Sleep better tonight | Vannacht **beter** slapen | infinitive headline |
| Get fit at home | Thuis **fit** worden | "fit" is native Dutch usage |
| Your money, organized | Je geldzaken op **orde** | idiom "op orde" |
| Never miss a bill | Nooit meer een **rekening** vergeten | — |
| Split bills with friends | Samen **delen**, zonder gedoe | *gedoe* = hassle, very Dutch |
| Edit like a pro | Bewerken als een **pro** | — |
| Learn a language | Leer **Spaans** in je eigen tempo | Dutch rarely need English lessons; name the target language |
| Plan your week in minutes | Je week **gepland** in een paar minuten | — |
| Amazing results | Gewoon **goede** resultaten | understatement converts better here |
| Shared grocery list | Boodschappen, **samen** geregeld | — |
| Your data stays private | Je gegevens blijven **privé** | accent required |

## Pitfalls

- **Hype words** (geweldig, fantastisch, ongelooflijk, revolutionair) trigger instant distrust.
- **False friends:**
  - *slim* (smart), *eventueel* (possibly), *actueel* (current), *brutaal* (cheeky)
  - *bekomen* (recover), *door* (through), *gift* (donation)
- **Flemish/Dutch mix-ups:** "gsm" (Flemish) vs "mobiel" (NL). For a shared listing, use neutral standard Dutch.
- **English word order from MT** ("Je kunt nu plannen je week") and overuse of "eenvoudig".
- **Misleading "gratis" and superlatives** are enforced by the ACM (consumer authority) and the Reclame Code Commissie.
