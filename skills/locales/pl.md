# Polish (Polski)

A large, Android-heavy, price-aware EU market with strong local champions (Allegro, InPost, BLIK). Polish readers notice English calques and gender slips immediately.

## Voice and register

- **"ty" (second person singular) is standard** in consumer app marketing, including most banks' apps and retailers.
- **"Pan/Pani" (formal)** fits government, healthcare, insurance, legal and some traditional banking. Follow the app's Polish UI.
- **Polish past tense and adjectives are gendered:** "zrobiłeś/zrobiłaś", "gotowy/gotowa".
  - Write headlines in the **present tense or imperative**, which are gender-neutral ("Oszczędzaj", "Planuj", "Zacznij").
  - Or use **noun phrases** ("Każdy wydatek pod kontrolą").
  - "Zaczynamy?" (inclusive *we*) avoids "Gotowy?".
- **Tone:** practical, friendly, a bit witty. Colloquial *ogarnąć* (sort out) works for casual brands ("Finanse ogarnięte"). Polish readers distrust exaggeration and love concrete value.

## Typography rules

- **Quotes:** „…” (low opening, high closing double quote); «…» nested. Never "…".
- **Dash:** spaced en dash (" – ").
- **Sentence case.** Diacritics ą ć ę ł ń ó ś ź ż must render. A font fallback on ł or ż is very visible. Missing diacritics ("zrob" for "zrób") are a typo, not a style.
- **Numbers:** "49,99 zł" (space before zł), comma decimal, space as thousands separator ("10 000"). Dates "4 listopada"; times "21:30".
- **Single-letter words:** words like *w, z, i, a, o, u* must not end a line (typographic "sierotki"). Bind them to the next word with an NBSP. This rule is widely observed in Polish publishing.

## Length

- Expect **+20–30% characters** versus English. Inflected forms are long ("zaplanowanych", "powiadomienia").
- Headline target ≤ 30 characters.
- Shorten with:
  - nominal "X pod kontrolą", "X bez Y" ("Trening bez siłowni")
  - imperative + noun
  - dropping "w prosty i szybki sposób"

## Store and search habits

- **Queries:**
  - money: "budżet domowy", "wydatki", "oszczędzanie"
  - health and fitness: "licznik kalorii", "dieta", "trening w domu", "krokomierz", "medytacja"
  - learning, food and home: "nauka angielskiego", "przepisy", "lista zakupów", "pogoda"
  - tools: "skaner"
- **Android dominates.** Play (pl-PL) often outweighs the App Store.
- **Payments and life:**
  - **BLIK** is the national mobile payment.
  - **InPost Paczkomat** lockers and **Allegro** shape e-commerce expectations.
  - Mention integrations only if real.
- **Price sensitivity:** "za darmo", "bez reklam", "bez zobowiązań" (no commitment) and "anulujesz w każdej chwili" convert when honest.
- **Local reference style:** Polish market leaders write short, friendly ty-imperatives with practical benefit framing and bold colour.

## Pitfalls

- **Gendered past tense or adjectives** aimed at the reader ("Zrobiłeś to!"). Rephrase ("Udało się!").
- **False friends:** *ewentualnie* (possibly), *aktualnie* (currently), *konkurs* (contest), *ordynarny* (vulgar, not ordinary), *fabryka* (factory, not fabric), *sympatyczny* (nice, not sympathetic).
- **Calques:** "zrobić sens", "mieć kontrolę nad" overused, "aplikacja, która pozwala Ci…". Rewrite as a direct benefit.
- **"Ty/Ciebie" capitalised mid-sentence** looks like a letter, not an ad. Lowercase in display copy unless the brand insists.
- **UOKiK (consumer protection office)** actively fines misleading subscription and "za darmo" claims, and unsubstantiated superlatives.
